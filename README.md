# FreshForge

FreshForge is an iOS app designed to help users track food expiration dates, organize their food, and reduce household food waste.

## Overview

FreshForge provides a visual way to keep track of food stored at home. Users can add food through receipt scanning or manual entry, organize items by expiration date, receive reminders, view food history, and explore recommendations for using food before it expires. Users can also manage grocery budgets.

## Features

* Receipt scanning and manual food entry
* Food expiration tracking
* Expiration reminders
* Visual food organization
* Food history
* Food recommendations
* Customizable themes and organization

## Development

FreshForge was developed in Swift using Xcode. I learned Swift through the Cooper Union STEM App Lab and applied what I learned to design and build the application.

The project was developed as an individual project and presented in a 96-team pitch competition.

## Recognition

Program instructors and the Cooper Union STEM App Lab coordinator recognized FreshForge as one of the program's most innovative app concepts.
